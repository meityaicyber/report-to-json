Summary
Introduction, objective, limitations and 
assumptions
Introduction
NorthQuill Cyber Assurance LLP performed a time-bound VAPT of the 
AsterLearn Campus LMS used by institutions for course delivery, 
student enrolment, assessments, grade management, assignment 
submissions and analytics dashboards.
Objective
The objective was to identify exploitable weaknesses before the next 
user-pilot release, validate access-control boundaries across student, 
teaching assistant, instructor and administrator roles, and provide 
actionable remediation guidance.
Limitations
The engagement was limited to the approved UAT environment, 
supplied roles and reachable workflows. No production systems, 
destructive testing, social engineering, denial-of-service testing or 
third-party SaaS penetration was performed.
Assumptions
Provided credentials represented intended role permissions. UAT 
configuration was assumed to be materially aligned to the planned 
release except where explicitly noted by the client.
Engagement conclusion
The LMS demonstrated a functional security baseline for authentication and transport security, but material weaknesses were 
identified in authorization enforcement, role assignment, stored content handling and several browser/API configuration controls. 
The three high-risk findings should be addressed before production release or broad user pilot.
Management risk statement
High overall risk due to confirmed cross-institution data exposure, role 
escalation and stored XSS impacting student/instructor trust 
boundaries.
Recommended release posture
Proceed only after P1 remediation has been verified and 
compensating monitoring is enabled for remaining P2/P3 items.
Primary affected control families
Access control, identity recovery, input/output handling, data export 
handling and security configuration.
