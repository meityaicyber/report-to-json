EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Executive Summary
Obsidian Ridge Cyber Assurance LLP performed a vulnerability assessment and penetration test of the FleetOps Partner Portal 
operated by HeliosFleet Logistics Pvt. Ltd. The application supports partner onboarding, shipment tracking, invoice review, claim 
attachments, delivery exception handling, and workflow approvals. The assessment identified authorization, authentication, 
browser security, and content handling weaknesses that should be remediated before the next production release.
Figure 1: Executive risk snapshot for open findings

The assessment team assessed the overall risk as HIGH. The primary risk theme is inconsistent authorization enforcement 
between UI-level controls and API-level services. The most significant findings were broken object-level authorization in invoice 
retrieval and privilege escalation through client-controlled role context. These issues can expose sensitive commercial data and 
bypass workflow approvals.

Key Business Risks

Cross-tenant data exposure: partner users may retrieve invoice and consignee data outside their authorized tenant boundary.

Workflow integrity gap: shipment release approvals can be manipulated by changing client-supplied role information.

Client-side trust weakness: stored content, browser headers, and CORS controls require hardening to reduce session-context 
abuse.

Identity lifecycle gap: account recovery and MFA controls require single-use and rate-limiting enforcement to maintain 
authentication assurance.

INTRODUCTION, OBJECTIVE, SCOPE & CONSTRAINTS
1. Introduction
HeliosFleet Logistics Pvt. Ltd. engaged Obsidian Ridge Cyber Assurance LLP to assess the security posture of the FleetOps Partner 
Portal. The portal enables third-party logistics partners to view shipments, raise claims, upload proof-of-delivery files, review 
invoices, and participate in dispatch release workflows.
2. Objective

Identify exploitable vulnerabilities in the web application, supporting REST APIs, session flows, and partner-facing 
workflows.

Validate whether authorization, authentication, input handling, file upload, and browser security controls are implemented 
consistently.

Provide prioritized, evidence-based observations with remediation guidance and retest criteria for engineering teams.

Map findings to recognized web application security standards and provide a practical remediation roadmap.
3. Limitations and Assumptions

Testing was performed against the UAT/staging environment using credentials and test data supplied by HeliosFleet.

No denial-of-service, destructive data modification, live payment authorization, or persistence beyond agreed test accounts 
was performed.

The application state, deployed code, and cloud gateway rules are assumed to match the UAT deployment available during 
the assessment window.

Third-party identity provider internals, managed cloud control plane, and vendor-hosted notification services were treated as 
trusted external dependencies unless behavior was observable through the application.
